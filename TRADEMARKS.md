## What you can do without asking

- Say that your product or service uses, runs on, is built from, or is compatible with Hangar.

- Distribute unmodified builds of Hangar under its name.
- Keep the name in the code, file names and package names of a fork, as long as the fork's README

  with your product, company or event.

To ask, open an issue on the [`hangar-dev/hangar`](https://github.com/hangar-dev/hangar)
repository.
